MCVS maintains two permanent background AtomicRunner threads that continuously generate and store weighted-matrix templates from the current root position into a shared vector database. This design deliberately eliminates the cold-start problem that affects pure online Monte-Carlo methods. The baseline agents (UCT, MAST, RAVE) receive no equivalent background knowledge accumulation. All agents are otherwise given identical thinking time (1 s) and the same maximum iteration budget per move.
